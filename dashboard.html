**Sports Digest — August 20, 2026** The Houston Texans absorbed a significant blow when second-year wide receiver Jayden Higgins tore his ACL during practice, ending his season before it began. Higgins was projected as the team's WR2, leaving Houston scrambling to address a suddenly thin receiver corps via trade or free agency. On the discipline front, the NFL fined the Cowboys and Saints $500,000 each following a series of fights during their joint practice sessions in Oxnard earlier this week. The league's action came the same day multiple players were ejected from a Giants-Dolphins joint practice after another brawl broke out, suggesting the preseason's physical edge is boiling over across the league. The Browns also suffered a scare when defensive end Alex Wright was carted off with a lower-leg injury during joint practice with the Bills. In other NFL notes, Tampa Bay's Vita Vea signed a one-year extension, the Commanders lost DT Johnny Newton to a pectoral injury requiring surgery, and two-time Pro Bowl cornerback Trevon Diggs spoke at his introductory Seahawks press conference. On the NHL side, Columbus Blue Jackets goaltender Elvis Merzlikins underwent shoulder surgery and is out indefinitely, adding to what has been a turbulent offseason for the franchise. The Red Wings completed a trade for a prospect center while continuing to navigate Dylan Larkin's reported trade request, with Minnesota remaining his preferred destination. The NHL also confirmed the Bruins and Oilers will play regular-season games in Germany in 2027, with Edmonton visiting Cologne and Boston heading to Munich.
